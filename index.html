FAQ

Quick answers

Is CoolingOff really free?

Yes. The whole extension is free, with no trial or limits. If it helps you, there's an optional “buy me a coffee” tip, never required.

Does it block me from buying?

No. It never stops a purchase. You choose to cool off, and you can always say “I'm buying this now”. It adds a pause, not a wall.

What data do you collect?

None. Your list is stored only in your browser. There are no accounts and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.

Which browsers and shops does it support?

Chrome, Edge and Firefox on desktop. It works on major shops like Amazon, Temu, AliExpress, Shein, Daraz, eBay and Shopify stores, and you can add any item by hand.

Does it work on my phone?

Not yet. It's a desktop browser extension. Install it on your computer, where most deliberate online shopping happens.
